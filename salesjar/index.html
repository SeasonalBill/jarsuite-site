What SalesJar does

SalesJar is for lawn care and home service companies that want a daily view of sales, cancels and remaining production, without building a separate report each morning. The figures are pulled from the office software every day.

  • Daily sales, month to date and year to date, by brand.
  • Sales pace versus goal, with selling days remaining.
  • Cancels today and year over year, so you can see what you kept.
  • Remaining production by service, toward a season end date.
  • Pulled from the office software every day.

Questions about SalesJar

What does SalesJar show?

Daily sales, month to date and year to date, by brand; sales pace versus goal, with selling days remaining; cancels today and year over year; and remaining production by service, toward a season end date.

Where do the numbers come from?

SalesJar is pulled from the office software every day. It is a dashboard for those figures, not a replacement for scheduling, routing or billing.

Is SalesJar a full office system?

No. It is a daily sales dashboard. Jar Suite is not scheduling, routing or billing software.

What does the $399 a month price include?

One flat monthly price for the dashboard: daily sales, pace versus goal, cancels and remaining production.
