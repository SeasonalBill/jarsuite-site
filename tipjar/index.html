What TipJar does

TipJar is for lawn care and home service companies that want a review ask to go out after a visit, instead of hoping the customer remembers to leave one. It is used at Seasonal Lawncare, including the review request a customer sees after a visit.

  • Review requests after a job, for Google and Facebook.
  • Post-service surveys, including an NPS-style score.
  • Technician scoreboards so the office can see how the asks are going.

TipJar also includes a post-call rating flow: a customer can rate their call with the office, and the office can see those ratings on a scoreboard by team member.

A referral add-on that lives with TipJar. Customers and techs get a personal link they can share themselves.

  • Personal referral links for customers and technicians.
  • The office confirms matches before a reward is due.
  • $49 a month, added to a TipJar plan.

How a review request works

TipJar handles the after-visit ask and gives the office a way to see how it is going.

  1. A visit is completed After a job, TipJar asks the customer for a Google or Facebook review and collects a post-service survey, including an NPS-style score.
  2. The customer leaves a review or a survey The customer sees a review request on their phone and can choose Google or Facebook. Surveys and post-call ratings are collected in TipJar.
  3. The office sees the scoreboards Technician scoreboards show how the asks are going. A separate scoreboard shows post-call ratings by team member.
  4. Optional: share a referral link If you add FriendJar, customers and technicians get a personal referral link. The office confirms matches before a reward is due.

Questions about TipJar

Does TipJar ask for Google and Facebook reviews?

Yes. After a visit, TipJar asks the customer for a Google or Facebook review. It also collects a post-service survey, including an NPS-style score.

What does the office see?

The office gets scoreboards by technician so you can see how the asks are going. There is also a scoreboard for post-call ratings, and a page a customer uses to rate their call with the office.

Can I use TipJar without FriendJar?

Yes. FriendJar is an optional add-on. TipJar on its own is review requests and post-service surveys. FriendJar adds personal referral links and is $49 a month on top of a TipJar plan.

Who confirms a FriendJar referral reward?

The office confirms matches before a reward is due. Customers and technicians get a personal link they can share themselves; the office still confirms the match.

What does TipJar cost?

TipJar is $449 a month. FriendJar is a separate $49 a month add-on and requires TipJar.

Can pest control companies use TipJar?

Yes. TipJar is review request software used at a lawn care company, and it can be used by pest control and other home service companies too. Request access and we will set up your account.
