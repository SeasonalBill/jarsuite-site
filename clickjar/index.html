Ad reporting dashboard for lawn care.

ClickJar shows what you spent, what leads came in, and how you are ranking, across the ad channels you run.

What ClickJar does

ClickJar is for lawn care and home service companies that want spend, leads, keywords and SEO tracking in one dashboard instead of checking each channel on its own. These screens are from Seasonal Lawncare and Mosquito Pros.

The spend view puts what you spent next to the leads that came in, including cost per lead and sales, across the ad channels you run. Keyword research is for the terms you care about. SEO tracking is for your pages, including where you are missing in local results. Website traffic can sit next to the ad numbers, and the dashboard can show recommended changes such as wasted spend on search terms.

  • Spend and leads in one view, across ad channels.
  • Keyword research for the terms you care about.
  • SEO tracking for your pages, including where you are missing in local results.

The dashboard can also show recommended changes, including wasted spend on search terms, and website traffic next to the ad numbers. See which calls those ads produced with RingJar.

ClickJar does not run your ads, and it is not a replacement for an advertising agency. It is a reporting dashboard. We set up outside companies one at a time; there is no self-serve sign-up. You can request ClickJar on its own, or pair it with RingJar if you also want call tracking by ad source. Lawn care and pest control companies can both request access. The $99 a month price is for the dashboard described on this page.

How to use ClickJar with your ads

ClickJar is a reporting dashboard for the ad channels you already run.

  1. See spend and leads together ClickJar shows what you spent and what leads came in, across ad channels, including cost per lead and sales on the spend view.
  2. Review keywords and wasted spend Use keyword research for the terms you care about, and review recommended changes such as wasted spend on search terms.
  3. Check SEO tracking SEO tracking shows how your pages are ranking, including where you are missing in local results. Website traffic can sit next to the ad numbers.
  4. Match ads to phone calls Pair ClickJar with RingJar if you want to see the calls that came from each ad source, and whether they were answered or missed.

Questions about ClickJar

What does ClickJar show?

Spend and leads in one view, across the ad channels you run, plus keyword research for the terms you care about and SEO tracking for your pages, including where you are missing in local results. It can also show website traffic next to the ad numbers, and recommended changes such as wasted spend on search terms.

Does ClickJar replace an advertising agency?

No. ClickJar is an ad reporting dashboard. It is a way to see spend, leads, keywords and SEO tracking in one place. It does not run your ads for you.

Can I see which ads made the phone ring?

Pair ClickJar with RingJar. ClickJar shows spend and leads. RingJar shows the calls that came from each ad source, and whether they were answered or missed.

Can pest control companies use ClickJar?

Yes. The screens on this page are from Seasonal Lawncare and Mosquito Pros. Request access and we will set up your account.
