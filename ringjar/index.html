What RingJar does

RingJar is for lawn care and home service companies that want to know which ads make the phone ring. It is not live GPS and it is not a full phone system. Calls still forward to your office line.

You give each ad source its own tracking number. When a customer calls that number, the call is forwarded to the office, and RingJar records the inbound call. The log marks the call answered or missed from the forward, and matches the caller to your customer list when it can, so you can tell new callers from existing customers. A summary shows calls, unique callers, and answered versus missed, by ad source.

How call tracking works

RingJar follows the call from the ad to the office line, then logs what happened.

  1. Give each ad source a tracking number Each tracking number belongs to one ad source, such as a campaign or listing, and forwards to your office line.
  2. The customer calls that number The call rings in the office. RingJar records the inbound call and uses the tracking number to tell which ad it came from.
  3. Answered or missed is logged The call log marks each inbound call answered or missed from the forward, and matches the caller to your customer list when it can.
  4. Review the summary by source A source summary shows calls, unique callers, and answered versus missed. Use ClickJar if you also want spend and leads in the same picture.

Questions about RingJar

Does the customer call a different number?

Each ad source gets its own tracking number. That number forwards to your office line, so the call still rings in the office. RingJar uses the tracking number to tell which ad the call came from.

Can RingJar tell new callers from existing customers?

Yes. RingJar matches the caller to your customer list when it can, so you can tell new callers from existing customers.

Is this GPS or a full phone system?

No. RingJar is call tracking. It is not live GPS, and it is not a replacement for your office phone system. It records inbound calls on each tracking number, marks them answered or missed from the forward, and shows a log and a summary by ad source.

How does RingJar work with ClickJar?

ClickJar shows ad spend and leads. RingJar shows the calls that came from each ad source. Used together, you can look at spend next to the calls those ads produced.
