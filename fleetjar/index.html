Jar Suite / FleetJar

Fleet management for lawn care trucks.

FleetJar keeps every truck's records in one place: service history, checklists, problems and repairs, tires, parts and paperwork. Each truck gets its own QR code, so a driver can scan it and open that truck's page.

FleetJar is for lawn care and home service companies that want truck records, checklists and paperwork in one place. It is not live GPS and it does not route crews. The sign-in screen on this page is from Seasonal Lawncare's live FleetJar app.

Truck records and QR codes

  • One page per truck with its photo, truck number, VIN and mileage.
  • A QR code for each truck, printable as cards in bulk. The code stays with the truck even if its number changes.
  • Service history (oil, filters, mileage, repairs) that is added to, never overwritten.

Checklists, problems and repairs

  • Truck checklists by season, with progress saved as drivers go.
  • Drivers report problems; the office turns them into work orders and tracks them to done.
  • A home screen with today's safety problems, overdue items and unfinished checklists.

Tires, parts and paperwork

  • Tires on each truck now, plus past replacements.
  • Parts stock, a shopping list and parts orders.
  • Invoices with PDFs, plates and expiration dates.
  • Spreadsheet and PDF import for your existing records.

Who sees what

  • Owner, Manager, Driver and read-only Guest roles.
  • Employees set their own passwords from a one-time setup code.
  • A Fleet Assistant that answers questions from your saved truck records.

How crews and the office use FleetJar

FleetJar is built around the truck page, not a live map.

  1. Each truck has a record and a QR code The office keeps a page per truck with its photo, number, VIN and mileage. Each truck gets a QR code, printable as cards in bulk, that opens that truck's page.
  2. Drivers run checklists and report problems Truck checklists are organized by season, and progress is saved as drivers go. Drivers report problems; the office turns them into work orders and tracks them to done.
  3. Service, tires and paperwork stay on the truck Service history is added to, never overwritten. Tires, parts, invoices, plates and expiration dates live with the truck. You can import existing records from a spreadsheet or PDF.
  4. The office sees what is overdue A home screen shows today's safety problems, overdue items and unfinished checklists. Owner, Manager, Driver and read-only Guest roles control who sees what.

Questions about FleetJar

Does FleetJar include GPS tracking?

No. FleetJar is truck records, QR codes, checklists, repairs, service history, tires, parts and plate expirations. It does not include live GPS or routing.

What is the QR code for?

Each truck gets its own QR code, printable as cards in bulk. A driver can scan it and open that truck's page. The code stays with the truck even if its number changes.

What is the difference between $9 per truck and $149 unlimited?

You can pay $9 per truck a month, or $149 a month for unlimited trucks. Both cover the same fleet tools: records, QR codes, checklists, repairs, tires, parts and paperwork.

Can a pest control company use FleetJar?

Yes. FleetJar is fleet management for lawn care trucks, and the same records, checklists and paperwork tools can be used for pest control and other home service fleets. Request access and we will set up your account.

Who can see what in FleetJar?

There are Owner, Manager, Driver and read-only Guest roles. Employees set their own passwords from a one-time setup code. A Fleet Assistant can answer questions from your saved truck records.
