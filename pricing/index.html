Questions about pricing

Can I buy just one Jar?

Yes. Each plan is priced on its own, except FriendJar, which is a $49 a month add-on and requires TipJar. Request access for the products you want and we will set up billing with you.

What does SalesJar cost?

SalesJar is $399 a month. It is a daily sales dashboard: daily sales, month to date, year over year, cancels and production, pulled from the office software every day.

Is FleetJar priced per truck?

You can pay $9 per truck a month, or $149 a month for unlimited trucks. FleetJar is truck records and maintenance tools, not GPS tracking.

Do these prices include sales tax?

Prices are in US dollars and do not include any sales tax that may apply.

Can I sign up myself online?

Not yet. Every button on this site is Request access. We are opening accounts to outside companies one at a time and will set up your account and billing with you.
