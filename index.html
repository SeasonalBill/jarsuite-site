Questions about Jar Suite

Can I use just one Jar?

Yes. Each Jar does one job. You can request access to one product, or to several. FriendJar is the exception: it is an add-on and requires TipJar.

How do outside companies get access?

We are opening accounts to outside companies one at a time. Request access, tell us your company and which Jars you want, and we will set up your account and billing with you.

Is QuoteJar only for lawn care?

Yes, for now. QuoteJar gives homeowners an instant lawn quote from an address. Mosquito and pest quoting is not part of QuoteJar yet. SalesJar, TipJar, ClickJar, RingJar and FleetJar can be used by lawn care, pest control and other home service companies.

Is this a full office system?

No. Jar Suite is not scheduling, routing or billing software. It is a set of single-job tools: instant lawn quotes, a daily sales dashboard, review requests, referrals, ad reporting, call tracking and truck records.

Want to use Jar Suite at your company?

We are opening accounts to outside companies one at a time. Request access and we will set up your account and billing with you.
