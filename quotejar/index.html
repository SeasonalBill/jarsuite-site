Your office gets the lead, and the spam filter for website leads is included in the $300 monthly price.

How an instant lawn quote works

This is the quote flow a homeowner uses on the website. Prices on the sample quote screen are examples.

  1. The homeowner enters an address They type an address on your website to start an instant lawn quote. They do not have to call the office first.
  2. QuoteJar suggests the property As they type, QuoteJar suggests the matching property so they can pick the right place.
  3. The lawn is measured The lawn is measured on an aerial photo as part of the quote. The homeowner can fix the outline if it needs a correction.
  4. They see a priced quote After the lawn is measured, they see a quote they can accept or book. Your office gets the lead. The lead spam filter is included in the monthly price.

Who QuoteJar is for

QuoteJar is for lawn care companies that want instant online lawn quotes on their own website. It is not a full office system, and it is not pest-quote software.

If you also want review requests, call tracking or ad reporting, those are separate Jars. You can request one product or several. We set up outside companies one at a time.

Questions about QuoteJar

Does the homeowner have to call first?

No. They start on your website by entering an address. QuoteJar suggests the matching property, measures the lawn as part of the quote, and shows a priced quote they can accept or book. Your office gets the lead.

Is the lawn measured as part of the quote?

Yes. QuoteJar measures the lawn on an aerial photo as part of the quote, so your office does not have to measure it first. The homeowner can fix the outline if it needs a correction.

Can QuoteJar quote mosquito or pest work?

Not yet. QuoteJar is lawn care instant quote software. Mosquito and pest quoting is not part of QuoteJar yet.

What is included in the $300 a month price?

One flat monthly price. The lead spam filter for website leads is included. There is not a separate spam-filter fee.

How do I put this on my website?

QuoteJar runs on your own website, so a homeowner can get a lawn quote there. We are opening accounts to outside companies one at a time. Request access and we will set up your account and billing with you.

Can I use QuoteJar with the other Jars?

Yes. You can request QuoteJar on its own, or pair it with TipJar for review requests and RingJar for call tracking. FriendJar is a TipJar add-on and is not required for QuoteJar.
